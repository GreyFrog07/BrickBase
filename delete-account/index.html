We're sorry to see you go. If you wish to delete your BrickBase account and all associated data, you can do so using either of the methods described below.

Option 1: Delete via Email

You can request account deletion by sending an email to our support team. Please include the mobile number or email address associated with your account so we can verify your identity.

Email: support@brickbase.co.in

Our team will process your request and confirm the deletion within 48 hours.

Option 2: Delete from the App

You can also delete your account directly from within the BrickBase app:

  • Open the BrickBase app and navigate to your Profile screen.
  • Scroll down to the bottom of the Profile screen.
  • Tap the Delete Account option.
  • Verify your identity via authentication.
  • Confirm the deletion to instantly remove your account and all associated data.

This process is immediate and cannot be undone.

What Data Gets Deleted?

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Your profile information (name, mobile number, email address).
  • All property listings you have created.
  • Photos and documents you have uploaded.
  • Your usage history and preferences.

Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered. Please make sure you want to proceed before confirming.

Need Help?

If you're facing any issues or have questions before deleting your account, feel free to reach out to us at support@brickbase.co.in. We're happy to help.
